Pain Points We Solve

Three Problems Hygiene Product Thermoformed Trays Buyers Face

🧴
Packaging Contamination — Hygiene Products Require Clean Packaging
Problem: Baby hygiene products (cotton pads, wipes, swabs) are skin-contact items — packaging contamination transfers to the product surface and potentially to baby skin. Standard thermoforming production environments may not provide adequate particulate cleanliness for hygiene product packaging. Solution: ISO 9001:2015 documented production with controlled material handling and clean production environment for all baby hygiene packaging. BPA-free materials confirmed per batch.
🌿
BPA-Free for Skin-Contact Hygiene Products
Problem: Baby hygiene products are applied to sensitive infant skin — packaging in contact with hygiene products must be free from BPA and phthalate plasticisers that could migrate to the product surface. Solution: BPA-free PP and BPA-free APET as standard for all baby hygiene packaging. Material CoA confirming BPA-free per production batch.
💦
Moisture Compatibility — Hygiene Products in Humid Environments
Problem: Baby wipes and wet cotton products generate moisture inside the packaging that affects packaging material performance over product shelf life. Solution: PP is the preferred material for moisture-exposed baby hygiene packaging — PP has very low moisture absorption and maintains dimensional stability in humid packaging environments.
Design Requirements

Hygiene Product Thermoformed Trays — Key Design Requirements

🏭
Clean Production Environment — ISO 9001:2015 Controlled
Baby hygiene packaging production under ISO 9001:2015 controlled procedures — documented material handling, production equipment cleaning, and final inspection before dispatch. ISO 9001:2015 batch documentation per run confirms production conditions for each baby hygiene packaging batch.
💦
Moisture-Compatible PP — For Wet Hygiene Product Applications
PP is preferred over APET for baby wipes and wet hygiene product packaging — PP has very low moisture absorption (0.01–0.03%) compared to APET (0.1–0.3%), maintaining tray geometry and dimensional stability in the moist product environment over shelf life.
🔍
Crystal-Clear APET for Retail Hygiene Display
For retail-facing baby hygiene product packaging (cotton swab display, cotton pad retail packs), APET crystal-clear thermoforming provides the product visibility that drives retail purchase. APET is selected for dry hygiene product packaging where moisture compatibility is not required.
📦
Dispensing Compatibility — Wipe Tray Geometry for Pop-Up Dispensing
Baby wipe tray geometry must be designed for compatibility with the pop-up dispensing mechanism — the tray must hold the wipe stack in correct orientation for sequential dispensing without tangling or stack collapse during the product shelf life.
