Product Not Visible in Opaque or Low-Clarity Cosmetics Packaging
Problem: Cosmetics in opaque or low-clarity packaging cannot communicate the product's texture, colour, and quality at the retail shelf — the consumer makes a blind purchase decision. Solution: APET crystal-clear thermoforming for all cosmetics retail packaging requiring product visibility — the product communicates its own quality at the shelf.
💎
Standard PP Undermines Premium Cosmetics Brand Identity
Problem: Premium cosmetics brands using standard PP transparent packaging inserts do not achieve the optical clarity depth that communicates premium brand positioning — PP haze reduces the product's visual premium. Solution: PETG premium clarity thermoforming for luxury and premium cosmetics brands. The superior clarity depth communicates that the brand is confident in its product quality.
🏪
Retail Shelf Presence — Transparent Packaging Outperforms Opaque
Problem: Opaque cosmetics packaging forces the consumer to rely on label description rather than product experience. At a competitive retail shelf, the consumer chooses the product they can see. Solution: Transparent APET and PETG cosmetics packaging — the product visible, the brand communicates through the product itself, not just the label.
Design Requirements

Transparent Thermoformed Cosmetic Packaging — Key Design Requirements

🔍
APET Clarity Grade Selection — Standard vs Optically Enhanced
APET is available in standard crystal-clear grade and optically enhanced clarity grade — the enhanced grade provides greater optical clarity depth at a slightly higher material cost. Grade selection confirmed from the cosmetics brand clarity requirement and OEM volume before tooling.
💎
PETG vs APET — The Visible Clarity Difference for Premium Cosmetics
PETG provides superior optical clarity over APET with a dimensional depth to the clarity that is visible at the retail shelf. For cosmetics brands where the packaging is a brand statement, PETG is the correct material. For cosmetics brands where packaging is functional retail display, APET crystal-clear is sufficient. Material recommendation made at brief stage.
🔒
Tamper-Evidence in Transparent Cosmetics Packaging
Transparent cosmetic blister packs and clamshell packaging provide tamper evidence — the heat-sealed blister or clamshell lock cannot be opened and resealed without visible evidence. Tamper-evident transparent cosmetics packaging communicates product integrity to the consumer.
🏪
Counter Display and Retail Display Format Design
Transparent cosmetics display trays and counter display units designed for the specific retail format — shelf standard, peg hanging, counter display, or in-store unit. Retail format specification confirmed from the brand's retail programme before design.
