Three Problems Lotion & Cream Tray Inserts Buyers Face

🎁
Poor Gift Set Presentation — Products Not Positioned at Best Brand Angle
Problem: Baby skincare gift set inserts that do not hold each product in label-visible orientation hide the product branding — a brand presentation failure for premium baby skincare gift sets. Solution: Cavity geometry designed for label-visible orientation for every product in the gift set — the product label faces the consumer through the gift box window at every viewing angle.
🌿
BPA-Free for Skin-Contact Baby Skincare Proximity
Problem: Baby lotion, cream, and oil packaging is in proximity to products applied directly to infant skin. BPA-free material specification is required for all baby skincare packaging contact applications. Solution: BPA-free APET and PETG for all baby skincare packaging. Material CoA confirming BPA-free status per production batch.
💫
Low-Clarity Packaging Undermining Premium Baby Skincare Brand
Problem: Baby skincare brands that position as premium or natural have their brand positioning undermined by low-clarity thermoformed packaging inserts — the lack of clarity communicates a standard, not premium, product. Solution: PETG premium clarity thermoforming for luxury baby skincare brand packaging — superior optical clarity over APET, premium surface finish, and the presentation quality that premium baby brands require.
Design Requirements

Lotion & Cream Tray Inserts — Key Design Requirements

🎁
Label-Visible Orientation — Every Product at Its Best Presentation Angle
Cavity geometry designed for label-visible orientation — the product label faces forward through the gift box window at the correct viewing angle. For cylindrical lotion bottles, the label-visible orientation is confirmed from the label position on the bottle before cavity design. All products in a gift set tray face the correct direction for brand communication.
💎
PETG Premium Clarity — Luxury Baby Brand Presentation
PETG crystal-clear thermoforming provides superior optical clarity over APET — the difference is visible at the retail shelf. For luxury baby skincare brand gift sets where packaging communicates brand positioning, PETG is the preferred material. APET is sufficient for mid-range baby skincare brands.
🔒
Cap and Pump Head Protection — Lotion and Cream Dispenser Integrity
Lotion pump heads and flip-top caps on baby skincare products must be protected from the force of other products in the gift set tray — contact or lateral load on a pump head can prime the pump and cause product leakage inside the gift box. Individual cavity design with cap clearance prevents pump priming and cap damage.
📦
Gift Box Compatibility — Tray Insert Dimensions to Box Interior
Baby skincare gift set tray inserts are designed to the specific gift box interior dimensions — the tray must sit flush in the box base, with product heights correctly positioned for the gift box window and lid. Box interior dimensions confirmed from the physical gift box before tooling commitment.
