Pain Points We Solve

Three Problems Makeup Kit Thermoformed Trays Buyers Face

📏
Height Variation — 12 to 36 Products at 10 to 140mm Height in One Tray
Problem: Makeup kit inserts containing mascara tubes (140mm), lipsticks (70mm), compacts (25mm), and eyeshadow palettes (15mm) in one tray present extreme height variation. Tall products visually dominate short products — the short products appear lost or minor. Solution: Height variation management through product grouping (tall products together, short products together) and fill technique (small platforms under short products to equalize visual height). Confirmed from the complete kit product list before design.
💅
Mirror Surfaces in Makeup Kits — No Contact with Reflective Surfaces
Problem: Makeup compacts with mirror lids, eyeshadow palettes with mirror panels, and bronzer compacts with mirror inlays must not contact the packaging tray surface — mirror contact causes micro-scratches that appear as haze in the mirror. Solution: Face-up cavity design for all products with mirror surfaces — products placed face-up in the tray with the mirror facing away from the cavity base. Powder product compacts face-up also prevents powder migration under vibration.
🎨
Complex Product Count — 20+ Products in One Tray Must Not Look Crowded
Problem: Professional makeup kit inserts with 20+ individual products risk looking overcrowded if cavity spacing is too tight — the tray looks like a product warehouse rather than a beauty kit. Solution: Visual density design — cavity spacing balanced between organization and visual breathing room. Products grouped by category with defined visual separation between groups.
Design Requirements

Makeup Kit Thermoformed Trays — Key Design Requirements

📏
Height Variation Management — Fill Technique and Product Grouping
Height variation in makeup kit trays is managed by two techniques: product grouping (tall products on one side, short products on another — creates visual rhythm) and fill technique (small thermoformed platforms under short products to equalize visual height at the tray top surface). Both techniques confirmed from the complete kit product list and the gift box window height before tooling.
💅
Face-Up Cavity Design for Mirror and Powder Products
All compact cases, eyeshadow palettes, and powder products are designed in face-up cavity position — the decorated lid or mirror faces away from the cavity base. Face-up positioning prevents mirror surface contact and prevents powder migration in pressed powder compacts during transit vibration.
🎨
Product Sequence Cavity Layout — Kit-Use Order
Professional makeup kit products are organized in the tray in kit-use sequence — the order a makeup artist or consumer would use them (prep → base → eyes → lips → set). This organization makes the kit more intuitive and communicates professional quality through thoughtful presentation.
💎
PETG for Luxury Makeup Gift Sets
Premium makeup gift sets use PETG thermoforming — superior clarity presents the full kit through the packaging with the visual quality that luxury beauty brand gift sets require.
