Automotive mechanical parts packaging presents challenges different from body component or interior packaging. The primary concern is machined surface protection — precision machined surfaces (bearing journals, sealing faces, mating surfaces) must be protected from contact, contamination, and damage during transit. The secondary concern is component weight — mechanical parts are substantially heavier than body components, requiring HDPE or heavy-gauge PP packaging capable of carrying the component weight without tray deformation under pallet stacking loads. Both requirements are addressed in the initial component brief.

Pain Points We Solve

Three Problems Mechanical Parts Packaging Buyers Face

⚙️
Machined Surface Damage — Precision Surfaces Rejected on Receipt
Problem: Bearing journals, sealing faces, and precision mating surfaces on machined automotive components sustain contact damage in transit — scratches, burrs, or deformation that cause dimensional rejection at OEM goods receipt. Solution: Cavity geometry designed with precision surface isolation — machined surfaces identified from the component drawing and provided with clearance cavities that prevent packaging contact. Cavity design documentation confirms surface isolation before tooling.
🏋️
Component Weight — Standard PP Packaging Deforms Under Heavy Mechanical Parts
Problem: Standard PP thermoformed trays deform under heavy mechanical components — engine blocks, gearboxes, differential housings — when palletised under stack loads. Tray deformation releases the component from its cavity, causing transit damage. Solution: HDPE heavy-gauge packaging for components over 5kg. HDPE maintains tray geometry under heavy component weight and pallet stack loads. Material selection confirmed from component weight and stack specification.
🛢️
Oil and Coolant Contamination — Material Compatibility with Automotive Fluids
Problem: Mechanical components coated in protective oil or coolant residue during manufacturing contact packaging materials that absorb or react with the fluid — causing packaging deformation, discolouration, and chemical contamination of the component. Solution: PP and HDPE are both chemically resistant to automotive oils, coolants, and greases. Material grade confirmed from the fluid type used in the manufacturing process.
Design Requirements

Mechanical Parts Packaging Design — Key Requirements

🔬
Precision Surface Isolation — Machined Faces Must Not Contact Packaging
Precision machined surfaces — bearing journals, sealing faces, threaded sections — are identified from the component technical drawing before cavity design. Each precision surface receives a clearance cavity in the packaging — the surface is suspended within the cavity with no packaging contact. Cavity clearance dimension confirmed from the surface flatness tolerance of the machined component.
🏋️
Heavy Component Support — Distributed Load Across Tray Base
Heavy mechanical components (engine blocks, transmission cases, differential housings) must be supported across the maximum possible contact area at the tray base — not point-supported. Point support under a heavy component causes stress concentration in the tray base, tray cracking under stack load, and potential component tipping. Base geometry provides full component base profile support.
🔩
Thread and Bore Protection — End Caps and Port Closures
Threaded connections, fluid ports, and precision bores on mechanical components are protected by integrated cavity geometry — thread-end cavities that prevent contact with threaded ends, port closure recesses that prevent fluid port contamination. For external thread protection, the cavity geometry provides thread-clearance without the expense of separate end caps.
🧪
Chemical Compatibility — Oil, Coolant, and Grease Resistance
Mechanical automotive components frequently carry protective oils, anti-corrosion fluids, or coolant residues from manufacturing. PP and HDPE are confirmed chemically resistant to standard automotive oils, coolants, transmission fluids, and greases. For specialist fluids or components with specific chemical environments, fluid type is confirmed at brief stage.
