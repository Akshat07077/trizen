Pain Points We Solve

Three Problems Gift Set Display Trays Buyers Face

🎁
Unboxing Experience Failure — Products Not in Premium Presentation
Problem: Cosmetics gift sets where the tray insert allows products to shift position, fall over, or present in incorrect orientation create a poor unboxing experience — damaging the brand impression at the most critical consumer touchpoint. Solution: Custom APET or PETG gift tray with precision cavity geometry for every product in the gift set. Products cannot move, tilt, or shift position in transit or at the point of opening.
📏
Mixed Product Height Variation in Gift Set Destroys Visual Balance
Problem: Beauty gift sets containing a 200mL body lotion alongside a 30mL serum alongside a 15g eye cream have extreme height variation — if not managed, tall products visually dominate the gift set presentation. Solution: Height variation management — product grouping and fill technique applied from the complete gift set product list before design. Visual balance confirmed from prototype before production.
💎
Low-Clarity Tray Insert Undermines Luxury Gift Set Positioning
Problem: Luxury cosmetics gift sets in opaque or standard-clarity PP trays have their premium positioning undermined — the tray communicates standard, not luxury. Solution: PETG premium clarity for all luxury cosmetics gift set trays. PETG crystal depth presents the gift set products with the visual quality the brand commands.
Design Requirements

Gift Set Display Trays — Key Design Requirements

🎁
Gift Box Compatibility — Tray Dimensions to Box Interior
Gift set display trays designed to specific gift box interior dimensions — tray must sit flush in the box base, products at correct height for box window, lid closure confirmed with all products at maximum height. Tray dimensions confirmed from physical gift box before tooling.
📏
Height Variation Management — Mixed Product Type Gift Sets
Gift sets combining bodycare (tall), skincare (medium), and colour cosmetics (short) require systematic height variation management. Product grouping by height category, fill platforms for short products, and visual balance confirmed from physical product layout before cavity design.
💎
PETG for Premium Gift Sets — Luxury Clarity Standard
PETG thermoforming for luxury cosmetics gift set trays — superior clarity presents the gift set with the visual premium quality the luxury brand commands. APET for mid-range cosmetics gift sets where cost efficiency is balanced with clarity requirements.
🔒
Product Position Lock — No Movement in Transit
Gift set display tray cavities designed so products cannot move, tilt, or shift during transit. Products locked in position by cavity geometry — the unboxing experience is identical whether the gift set was just packed or has been in transit for 2 weeks.
