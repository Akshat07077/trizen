Assembly Line & Industrial Movement Trays — Robotic Pick, JIT Delivery & Inter-Plant Transit from Vapi

Trizen Packaging manufactures assembly line and industrial movement trays for automotive OEM production from our ISO 9001:2015 certified facility in Vapi, Gujarat. Automotive assembly tray OEM from Vapi — thermoformed trays for robotic pick-and-place assembly stations, JIT component delivery systems, inter-plant movement and transit, and multi-component kit assembly presentation. As an assembly line trays manufacturer from India serving automotive OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers, we produce trays with the dimensional consistency, cycle life durability, and documentation quality that automotive production programmes require.

Assembly line thermoformed trays are production tools, not standard packaging. The distinction matters: a standard protective tray holds a component safely. An assembly line tray holds the component at a defined X-Y position corresponding to the robotic pick coordinate — consistently, across every tray in the production batch, and across every batch in the OEM programme. This dimensional consistency requirement is what makes automotive assembly line trays a specialist thermoforming application.

Pain Points We Solve

Three Problems Automotive Assembly Line Packaging Buyers Face

🤖
Robotic Pick Errors from Dimensional Drift Between Batches
Problem: Robotic pick coordinates are programmed to the prototype tray geometry. When thermoforming supplier batches show dimensional drift — even by 1–2mm — the robot misses the pick, causing assembly line stoppages. Solution: In-house CNC tooling at Vapi with ISO 9001:2015 dimensional inspection per batch. Same tool, same material grade, same process parameters on every repeat production run. Dimensional check records supplied per batch.
📦
Component Damage During Inter-Plant Transit and Supplier-to-Line Movement
Problem: Automotive components shipped in inadequate transit trays suffer surface damage, deformation, and part-to-part contact damage — rejected on receipt and traced back to packaging supplier. Solution: HDPE inter-plant movement trays with custom cavity geometry designed from the specific component profile. HDPE tray geometry maintains under palletised stack loads that would deform standard PP trays.
🔧
Kit Assembly Errors from Incorrect Component Positional Organisation
Problem: Sub-assembly kits with components in wrong positional order cause assembly errors at the line station — the operator or robot picks the wrong component for the sequence. Solution: Kit assembly presentation trays designed with each component in its specific assembly-sequence position. Component positions confirmed from the assembly sequence documentation before tooling.
Design Requirements

Assembly Line Tray Design — Key Requirements

📐
Dimensional Consistency — ±0.5mm Across Full OEM Production Volume
Robotic assembly applications require tray cavity position to ±0.5mm across the full production batch — not just the prototype approval sample. This requires CNC-tooled thermoforming, controlled process parameters, and dimensional inspection of every batch. Standard thermoforming without these controls cannot reliably hold this tolerance across 10,000+ tray production runs.
🔄
Cycle Life — Designed for 300 to 500+ Return Cycles
Automotive returnable trays must maintain dimensional accuracy across 300 to 500 or more use cycles — loading, transit, line feeding, emptying, return, and restacking. HDPE is the preferred material for high-cycle returnable applications. PP is suitable for lower cycle count programmes. Material selection and wall thickness confirmed from the cycle life requirement.
📦
Stack Load Geometry — Tray Must Not Deform Under Pallet Load
Assembly line trays are stacked 10 to 20 high on pallets in transit and at line-side storage. The tray geometry must support the stack load without tray deformation — base wall thickness, rib geometry, and stacking ledge design all contribute to stack load performance. Confirmed from the tray size, component weight, and stack height specification.
🤖
Robotic Pick Geometry — Cavity Position, Depth, and Extraction Force
Robotic pick-and-place requires not just correct cavity position but correct cavity depth and extraction force — the robot gripper must be able to extract the component cleanly without the cavity grip force causing extraction failure. Cavity geometry designed in coordination with the robotic pick system specification.

A JIT delivery tray is designed for the supplier-to-assembly-line direct feed system — the component leaves the supplier in the tray and goes directly to the line station without repacking. It must be returnable (for the supplier loop), correctly oriented for direct line feed, and sized for the line station rack system. A transit tray is designed for inter-plant movement with protection as the primary requirement. Both are thermoformed — but the JIT tray is designed from the line station specification, while the transit tray is designed from the logistics chain specification.
